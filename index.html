Our Process

Every job follows the same four-step process. We respect your time, and deliver a finish you'll be excited about.

01

Free Estimate

Paul or Roman will walk your property, listen to what you're hoping to achieve, and talk through colors, surfaces, and timeline. You'll get a clear, no-obligation estimate, just an honest number based on the work in front of us.

02

Prep

Great paint jobs start with the prep work, and we don't cut corners here. That means cleaning and repairing surfaces, sanding, patching, taping, and protecting your landscaping, windows, and fixtures.

03

Paint

With surfaces prepped and protected, we get to work. We use premium materials and proven techniques suited to your surface whether it's brick, siding, stucco, trim, interior walls, or whatever the job calls for. Clean lines, even coverage, and attention to detail from start to finish.

04

Quality Check

Before we consider the job done, we walk it with you. We check every wall, edge, and corner against our own high standard, not just yours. Then we make any final touch-ups on the spot if needed. We don't leave until it's right.
