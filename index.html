How a project with us goes

Four stages, the same crew from the first walkthrough to the last one.

01

Consult

We walk the property, talk through colors and finishes, and send a written estimate within a couple of days.

02

Prep

Washing, scraping, sanding, and taping. Prep is most of the job, and where most shortcuts happen.

03

Paint

Two coats of a coating built for the surface and the Florida climate, applied start to finish by the same crew.

04

Walkthrough

We walk the property with you before we call it done, and fix anything that doesn't meet the mark.
